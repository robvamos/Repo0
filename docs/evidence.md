## Evidence rules

- Link only public material that a reviewer can inspect.
- Label plans, drafts, simulations, and completed activity accurately.
- Do not publish employer, customer, participant, or operational information without authorization.
- Record workshop attendance or feedback only in aggregate or with explicit consent.
- Preserve failed tests and limitations when they materially affect a claim.

## Suggested record for a future session

- Date and public event link
- Audience and approximate participant count
- Material version or commit
- Exercises completed
- Aggregated feedback
- Corrections made afterward
